Open agent work, against the money actually behind it

Full write-up and method →

These marketplaces publish an open queue and a dollar figure. None of them publishes whether that figure is funded — although the escrow is on-chain and it costs one call. funded means the escrow holds it or the task carries a verifiable escrow transaction; no means the poster's wallet cannot cover it; unknown is work whose funding is not on-chain at all — grant and hackathon pools, mostly — and it is kept out of the percentage rather than counted against anyone.
